Slack bot

Lets your team ask Broppy in Slack with /broppy. Adding it connects Slack for reading too.

Before you add it
  • A Slack workspace belongs to one Broppy company at a time. To move it, disconnect the bot in the other company first.
  • You need permission to install apps in your Slack workspace, or your Slack admin's approval.
  • Broppy reads the messages in every channel you invite it to. Invite it with /invite @Broppy.
  • Each teammate's Slack email must match their Broppy account for Broppy to answer them.
  • One Slack workspace per Broppy company. To remove the bot completely, also uninstall it in Slack.

Slack tasks to Jira

Choose a Jira project for a Slack channel and Broppy offers Track in Jira when people there agree on a task. Only the reviewed summary goes to Jira. The people in the channel when you save are the ones who see the Jira link; save again after people join.

AI model

Broppy sends only the passages a person is allowed to see, with their question, to the model you choose. Your API key is stored encrypted and never shown again.
